Plant Leaf Disease Detection

A classification system that identifies plant diseases directly from leaf images, aimed at helping farmers catch problems before they spread. Images are preprocessed and reduced to their most informative features — color and texture — before a K-Nearest Neighbors classifier sorts each leaf as healthy or diseased. The result is a lightweight, explainable approach that prioritizes early detection over complexity.
